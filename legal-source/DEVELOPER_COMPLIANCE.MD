15. AI INTERVIEW START SCREEN
This is mandatory from a product-compliance perspective.
Where
Before the user's first interaction with the AI interviewer.
The user should see the disclosure before starting.
Display
AI Interview Simulator
You are about to interact with an AI-powered interview simulator. Questions, scores
and feedback may be generated using artificial intelligence and may contain errors.
This experience is for training and self-assessment only. It is not an official
interview or hiring decision.
Button:
Start AI Interview
Article 50 AI Act requires people interacting directly with qualifying AI systems to be informed that they
are interacting with AI unless it is obvious. The Commission's current guidance says the disclosure
should occur from the start of the first interaction and be clear and distinguishable.
