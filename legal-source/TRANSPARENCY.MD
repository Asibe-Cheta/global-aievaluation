Global Ready AIEval does not use its AI interview simulator to perform emotion recognition or biometric

The Service does not intend to determine characteristics such as a user's personality, trustworthiness,
employability or professional suitability based on facial expressions, biometric characteristics or
inferred emotions.
If functionality involving biometric or emotion-related processing were ever considered in the future, it
would be subject to separate legal, data-protection and AI-governance assessment before
implementation.
